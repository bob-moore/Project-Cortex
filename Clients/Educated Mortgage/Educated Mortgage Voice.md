# Educated Mortgage — Voice

Read by every content/copy skill before drafting for this client, alongside [[Educated Mortgage Brand]]. Built from the staging rebuild (educatedmortgage.mdmserver.us) compared against the legacy live site (educatedmortgage.com).

## Format & Style Defaults

Agency-wide baseline (see `templates/Client Voice.md`). **Two documented exceptions for this client** — see Client-Specific Format Notes below: light exclamation points in headlines/CTAs only, and occasional em dashes for asides. Both are real, observed patterns from existing staging copy, not oversights — the exception overrides the default in those specific contexts only (body copy still follows the no-exclamation/no-em-dash defaults).

**Tone:** Simple and authentic.

**Reading Level:** Below 8th grade. Aim for 6th or 7th.

**Avoid:**
- Cringe or hype language
- Sales language
- Exclamation points (except the headline/CTA exception noted below)
- Colons used as sentence separators
- Repeating yourself
- Em dashes as separators or for emphasis, outside the asides exception noted below. Use a period or comma instead.
- Hyphens as separators. Use a period or comma instead.
- Years in claims (like "best XXX of 2026") unless the year is actually needed, like news. It ages badly.

**Dashes — when they ARE allowed:**
- Ranges and comparisons: $2-$4, 30-60 minutes, ages 25-40
- Compound/hyphenated words: pre-filled, well-known, long-term, evidence-based

**Text Format:**
- Prefer short sentences and short paragraphs with room to breathe. Vary the length, don't lock into one rhythm.
- Conversational, human language, not corporate copy.
- Use lists only when a list actually helps. Don't reach for one by default.
- Use H2 and H3 for major sections.
- No orphaned headings. If a paragraph doesn't follow a heading, cut the heading.
- Mix in some longer sentences with conjunctions and subordinate clauses, but keep short ones as the backbone.
- Define new terms when introduced.
- Vary the mood throughout the writing.

## Client-Specific Format Notes

- **Sentence length:** Short to medium, benefit-first. Headlines lead with the customer's situation ("Don't wait! Find out about your options today!") rather than the company.
- **Punctuation habits:** Light use of exclamation points in headlines/CTAs only ("Don't wait!", "Call Us!"), not in body copy — an intentional exception to the agency default. Em dashes used sparingly on staging copy for asides — also an exception, keep body copy dash-free otherwise.
- **Structural habits (lists vs. prose):** Short bulleted lists for "who this is for" segments (first-time buyers, competitive-market buyers, refinancers, unique scenarios) and for process steps (Getting Started → Review → Pre-Approval → Closing). Prose paragraphs for reassurance/trust copy.
- **Headline style:** Direct and benefit-first ("Mortgage guidance for every stage of homeownership," "Clear advice. Smart options. A team that stays with you.")
- **What this brand avoids:** Hard-sell urgency ("act now," countdown language), jargon without explanation, generic stock-lender phrases that could apply to any company.

## Brand-Specific Rules

**Brand voice:** Warm, plain-spoken, local, patient

**This brand sounds like:** A Madison-based lender who answers the phone personally, explains loan options without jargon, and treats the relationship as ongoing ("clients for life"), not transactional.

**Preferred terminology:**

| Say | Not |
|-----|-----|
| Mortgage Banker | "just a broker" |
| Dan "The Mortgage Man" O'Brien | Dan (alone, in bios/formal copy) |
| Cash Advantage Program | Ca$h Advantage (legacy stylization — confirm before reusing, see [[Educated Mortgage Brand]]) |
| pre-approved / pre-approval | pre-qual used interchangeably with pre-approval (they're different steps — don't blur them) |
| local Madison office, local market experts | generic "nationwide lender" framing |

**Additional rules observed from site copy / client feedback:**

- Lead with the phone number. 608-834-5000 appears in the header, hero, and multiple CTAs on every page — treat it as a near-mandatory element of any new page or landing page, not an afterthought in the footer.
- Testimonials are a first-class content type, not filler. They name staff by name (Dan, Heidi, Craig, Travis, Joe) — when excerpting or paraphrasing, preserve that specificity rather than genericizing to "our team."
- Use conditional, honest qualification language for financial claims: "you may not need 20% down," "some buyers may qualify" — never a blanket promise ("no down payment required").
- FAQ answers should teach, not deflect — match the pattern already on staging (e.g. "The best loan depends on your credit, down payment, monthly budget, and how long you plan to stay in the home. We'll walk you through your options...").
- Keep licensing/NMLS disclosure language exact and unabridged wherever it appears (see [[Educated Mortgage Brand]] Section 7) — this is compliance text, not marketing copy, and should not be rewritten for tone.
- Any SMS/lead-form consent copy must match the TCPA-compliant language already in use (see [[Educated Mortgage Brand]] Section 7) — don't loosen or shorten it for brevity.
