## 2. Target Audience

- **Primary audience:** Wisconsin (and some Illinois) home buyers and homeowners — first-time buyers, buyers refinancing, and buyers in competitive-offer situations. Geographic reach extends well beyond Madison: the live site targets 18+ WI cities (Appleton, Brookfield, Eau Claire, Green Bay, Kenosha, Milwaukee, Racine, Waukesha, and more) via WHEDA first-time-buyer landing pages, currently being consolidated into a single page per the sitemap project in Drive.
- **Pain points:** Not knowing which loan program fits (FHA/VA/conventional/USDA/WHEDA/jumbo), assuming 20% down is required, needing to move fast in a competitive market without a home sale contingency, distrust built up from a bad experience with a previous lender or big bank/credit union.
- **Sophistication level:** Mixed, skews first-time/less experienced. FAQ and Glossary of Terms sections exist specifically to explain jargon in plain language. Don't assume mortgage literacy.
- **Audience language (VOC):** Rich source available — dozens of Google/testimonial reviews on-site. Recurring themes: relationship and responsiveness ("picks up the phone"), speed under pressure (tight timelines, competing offers), staff named individually and thanked by name (Dan, Heidi, Craig, Travis, Joe), small-team trust ("smaller team of only 3 or 4 people... always someone available"), education/hand-holding for first-timers ("explained every step," "answered my dumb questions").

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone

Full rules in [[Educated Mortgage Voice]]. Summary:

- **Voice in 3–5 words:** Warm, plain-spoken, local, patient
- **This brand sounds like:** A local lender who picks up the phone and explains things clearly, not a call-center or a national bank
- **This brand never sounds like:** Corporate finance jargon, hard-sell urgency, or a faceless national lender
- **Formality:** Casual-professional, second person ("you"), contractions fine, phone number as a near-constant CTA (608-834-5000)
- **Sentence style:** Short, reassuring, benefit-first headlines ("Don't wait — find out about your options today!")
- **Sample approved copy (staging site):** *"We process, underwrite, and fund all loans right here in our Madison office, ensuring faster service, personalized support, and lending decisions made by experts who understand our local market."*
- **Sample rejected copy:** Old-site keyword-stuffed meta descriptions and title tags (e.g. a meta-keywords tag with 100+ stuffed terms) — being cleaned up in the rebuild, don't reintroduce that pattern

- **Product/feature names (exact casing):** Purchase, Refinance, Cash Advantage Program, Fixed-Rate Loans, Adjustable-Rate Loans, Conventional Loans, FHA Loans, VA Loans, USDA Loans, WHEDA Loans, Home Equity Loans, Bridge Loans
- **Competitor names:** None named on-site; audience language suggests comparison against big banks/credit unions and "another lender" who dropped the ball, not a named local competitor
- **Industry terms to use:** WHEDA, NMLS, pre-approval, pre-qualification — always paired with a plain-language explainer (Glossary of Terms exists for this)
- **Industry terms to avoid without explanation:** underwriting, escrow, amortization, contingency — fine to use but should be explained or linked to the glossary on first use in a page

## 5. SEO Requirements

- **Keyword strategy:** Local + loan-type combinations — "[city] mortgage," "Madison FHA loan," "VA loan Wisconsin," "mortgage rates Madison," "first time home buyer Wisconsin." Old site's URL/meta pattern confirms this; new site is consolidating near-duplicate city pages (WHEDA landing pages for 18+ cities) into fewer, stronger pages per the sitemap doc in Drive.
- **Example keywords:** Wisconsin mortgage, Madison FHA loan, Madison VA loan, WHEDA first time home buyer, mortgage rates Madison, cash offer mortgage
- **Dominant search intent:** Mixed commercial/informational — loan program pages and calculators are commercial-adjacent, FAQ/Glossary/Blog are informational, all funneling to the phone number or the mortgage-analysis lead form

## 7. Compliance, Legal & Claims

> [!danger] NMLS licensing disclosures are non-negotiable
> Every page needs accurate footer licensing text: WI Mortgage Banker License #222652BA and Mortgage Broker License #211655BR, Illinois Residential Mortgage Licensee License #MB.6760468 (with IDFPR address/phone), and the Equal Housing Opportunity logo. Don't drop or paraphrase these when redesigning page templates.

- Keep Dan's personal NMLS ID (222714) and the company NMLS# (222652) distinct and accurate — don't conflate them in bios or footers.
- Any SMS/text lead-gen copy must follow the TCPA Compliance Readiness Guide on file in Drive (Website folder): visible checkbox (not pre-checked), clear consent language, message-frequency and data-rate disclosures, STOP/HELP opt-out instructions, consent not a condition of purchase. The current lead form's consent text is the reference example.
- **Client-supplied SMS T&C and Privacy Policy pages (document-sourced, 2026-07-07):** Dan O'Brien sent finished copy for two required pages — SMS Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy — needed for Twilio SMS approval, to be linked in the footer sitewide. Source docx attachments are in Bob's inbox (see [[Educated Mortgage]] Open Items); pull verbatim rather than drafting fresh copy — this is attorney-reviewed compliance text, not marketing copy.
- BBB-accredited — BBB seal appears on-site; don't make claims that would jeopardize that standing.
- Avoid definitive rate/qualification promises in marketing copy ("you may not need 20% down," "some buyers may qualify" — conditional language already used correctly on staging, keep that pattern).
