## 2. Target Audience

- **Primary audience:** Commercial furniture dealers (local/regional/national) and end users — facility managers, project managers, procurement leads at businesses, schools, healthcare systems, government agencies
- **Pain points:** Installation crews that don't communicate; projects running over schedule; crews untrained on the client's specific furniture systems; having to manage multiple vendors across receiving, storage, and install
- **Sophistication level:** Experienced B2B buyers — comfortable with industry terms (FF&E, workstations, restacks, decommissioning); expect a professional partner, not a sales pitch
- **Audience language (VOC):** Real buyer questions sourced from Quora/competitor FAQs/industry blogs, organized by segment (dealers, end users, schools, healthcare/government, decommissioning) — full list in `context.md` in the WP repo. Top vetting question from dealers: *"Are your crews trained on [specific manufacturer's] systems?"*
- **Secondary audience:** Larger enterprise/out-of-region organizations wanting a WI/Northern IL installation partner — care about scalability and multi-site/multi-phase track record

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone

- **Voice in 3–5 words:** Approachable, expert, direct, reliable
- **This brand sounds like:** A seasoned regional partner who's done thousands of projects and doesn't need to oversell — confident enough to let the work speak, accessible enough that a first-time client feels comfortable reaching out.
- **This brand never sounds like:** A consumer furniture retailer, a corporate buzzword machine, or an overpromising startup. Never vague or inspirational at the expense of saying what they actually do. (The current live hero — "WE BUILD WHAT INSPIRES YOU" / "We Construct Your Vision" — is the brief's own textbook example of what to avoid.)
- **Formality / humor / sentence style / POV:** Professional; no humor; mix of punchy headline claims and fuller service-description sentences; first person "we" / second person "you"
- **Sample approved copy:** "SFI provides professional commercial furniture installation and delivery services throughout Wisconsin and Northern Illinois. Our experienced crews receive, transport, assemble, and install office furniture, workstations, private offices, conference rooms, educational furnishings, healthcare furniture, and FF&E products. Whether your project involves a small office reconfiguration or hundreds of workstations across multiple locations, our team delivers seamless installation services from receiving through final placement."
- **Sample rejected copy:** "WE BUILD WHAT INSPIRES YOU" (too vague, consumer-facing); "We Construct Your Vision" (doesn't say what they do or who they serve); "We pride ourselves on our experience and resourcefulness" (soft filler — lead with proof, not self-praise)

## 5. SEO Requirements

- **Keyword strategy:** Commercial-intent, geo-modified keywords reflecting WI/Northern IL coverage. Prioritize bottom-of-funnel terms; write for humans first, no keyword stuffing.
- **Example keywords:** Commercial furniture installation Wisconsin, office relocation Madison WI / Milwaukee WI, FF&E installation Wisconsin, commercial moving Wisconsin, furniture warehousing Wisconsin, school furniture installation Wisconsin, healthcare furniture installation Wisconsin
- **Dominant search intent:** Mix of informational and bottom-of-funnel — dealers looking for an installation partner, or end users comparing providers before requesting a quote
- **Domain authority context:** Established domain (~2020). Build regional authority with geo-modified mid/long-tail terms before targeting broad national terms.
- **On-page SEO rules:** Primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, ≥1 H2, meta title, meta description. Meta title format `[Primary Keyword] | Systems Furniture Installations` (≤60 chars). Meta description action-oriented, includes keyword, soft CTA ("Request a quote"), 150–155 chars. 2–3 internal link suggestions per page/post. Flag LocalBusiness/FAQ/Service schema opportunities. Reference cities naturally (Wisconsin, Northern Illinois, Madison, Milwaukee, Green Bay, etc.).

## 6. Content Types & Formats

- **Website/service page copy:** Lead with what the service is + who benefits + geographic scope. Include specific capabilities (not vague promises), industries served, scale range. Primary CTA sitewide: "Request a Quote."
- **Blog posts:** 1,000–1,800 words. Intro (problem/question) → H2 body sections → conclusion + CTA. One contextual mid-article CTA where relevant. Accessible-professional reading level. High-value topic list (buyer-intent questions SFI can own) documented in `context.md` in the WP repo — e.g. "What questions should I ask a commercial furniture installer?", "Can you install furniture in an occupied office without disrupting operations?"
- **Landing pages:** Pain point first, not product. One primary CTA per section.
