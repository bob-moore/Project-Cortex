# Systems Furniture Installations — Voice

Read by every content/copy skill before drafting for this client, alongside [[Systems Furniture Installations Brand]].

## Format & Style Defaults

Agency-wide baseline (see `templates/Client Voice.md`). No conflicts observed — this client's existing move away from exclamation-heavy copy already aligns with the default.

**Tone:** Simple and authentic.

**Reading Level:** Below 8th grade. Aim for 6th or 7th.

**Avoid:**
- Cringe or hype language
- Sales language
- Exclamation points
- Colons used as sentence separators
- Repeating yourself
- Em dashes as separators or for emphasis. Use a period or comma instead.
- Hyphens as separators. Use a period or comma instead.
- Years in claims (like "best XXX of 2026") unless the year is actually needed, like news. It ages badly.

**Dashes — when they ARE allowed:**
- Ranges and comparisons: $2-$4, 30-60 minutes, ages 25-40
- Compound/hyphenated words: pre-filled, well-known, long-term, evidence-based

**Text Format:**
- Prefer short sentences and short paragraphs with room to breathe. Vary the length, don't lock into one rhythm.
- Conversational, human language, not corporate copy.
- Use lists only when a list actually helps. Don't reach for one by default.
- Use H2 and H3 for major sections.
- No orphaned headings. If a paragraph doesn't follow a heading, cut the heading.
- Mix in some longer sentences with conjunctions and subordinate clauses, but keep short ones as the backbone.
- Define new terms when introduced.
- Vary the mood throughout the writing.

## Client-Specific Format Notes

- **Sentence length:** Mixed — short, punchy claims in headlines/hero copy; fuller explanatory sentences in service descriptions and proof points
- **Punctuation habits:** Standard professional punctuation; no exclamation-heavy copy (current live site overuses "!" in body copy — e.g. "we will keep it safe until you are ready!" — move away from this toward a calmer, more confident register)
- **Structural habits:** Service pages built from labeled blocks (What it is + who it's for → key capabilities → why SFI → CTA); FAQ content in inverted-pyramid/AEO format (question as heading, direct answer first); blog posts lead with the reader's problem
- **Headline style:** Benefit- or capability-first, not vague/inspirational. Hero H1 max 8 words, clear statement of what SFI does + where.
- **What this brand avoids:** Consumer/residential framing, hype words, vague "vision"/"inspire" language, self-praise without specifics ("we pride ourselves on..."), unsupported "full service" claims not backed by a concrete list

## Brand-Specific Rules

**Brand voice:** Approachable, expert, direct, reliable

**This brand sounds like:** A seasoned regional partner who's done thousands of projects and doesn't need to oversell — confident enough to let the work speak, accessible enough that a first-time client feels comfortable reaching out.

**Preferred terminology:**

| Say | Not |
|-----|-----|
| Commercial Furniture Installation & Delivery | Delivery & Installation |
| Office Relocation & Commercial Moving | Office Relocation |
| Commercial Furniture Warehousing, Receiving & Storage | Storage & Warehousing |
| Furniture Project Management & Logistics | Project Management |
| Furniture Decommissioning, Recycling & Asset Recovery | E-Waste Recycling |
| Demountable Walls | Modular Walls / DIRTT |
| Over 20 years in business | 15 years in business |
| New Berlin | Brookfield |

**Additional rules observed from site copy / client feedback:**

- Lead every page with what SFI does and who it's for — never open with a mission/vision statement before establishing the concrete offer
- Say "commercial" explicitly and often; the client's #1 kickoff note was that the site "needs to scream that we are commercial" — don't rely on implication
- Name the service area plainly (Wisconsin + Northern Illinois; note NE Iowa/Michigan reach only came up verbally, not yet client-approved for site copy — confirm before publishing)
- Emphasize independence — SFI is not tied to any single manufacturer or dealer; this is a trust signal, not a throwaway line
- Keep CTA language consistent sitewide: "Request a Quote" (or "Request a Free Quote" on buttons, 3–5 words)
- Don't reuse or invent testimonials — see Compliance section of [[Systems Furniture Installations Brand]]
- Alt text should be specific and situational, e.g. "SFI crew installing office workstations at corporate office in Madison, WI" — not generic
