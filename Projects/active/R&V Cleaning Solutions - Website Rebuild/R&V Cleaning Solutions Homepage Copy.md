**Intro:**
A few things property managers and homeowners often ask before booking with us.

- **Can you handle multiple units at once?** Yes. We regularly clean multiple units for turnover between leases, and can coordinate a schedule around your move-out and move-in dates.
- **Do you offer ongoing service for offices or buildings?** Yes. We offer recurring cleaning plans for offices and commercial buildings, from daily to monthly.
- **Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?** No. Our team brings professional supplies and equipment. If you would rather we use your own supplies, just let us know.
- **Do I need to be home during the cleaning?** No. You are welcome to be home, but many clients give us access instructions and go about their day.
- **Are your cleaners insured?** Yes. Our team is trained and insured.
- **Do you work around pets?** Yes. Let us know about any pets in the home so we can plan around them.
- **What if I am not happy with the cleaning?** Let us know and we will make it right.
