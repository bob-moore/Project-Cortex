# Systems Furniture Installations — Homepage Rewrite

Bounded engagement: homepage copy overhaul for [[Systems Furniture Installations]] (client profile, Brand, Voice, Stack live in `Clients/Systems Furniture Installations/`). Not a rebuild — enhancement work layered onto the existing WordPress site as part of the ongoing SEO/CRO retainer.

## Engagement — Homepage Rewrite (current focus)

Primary active deliverable, per the 2026-06-15 kickoff meeting: rewrite the homepage so it reads unmistakably **commercial** (current live copy — "WE BUILD WHAT INSPIRES YOU" / "We Construct Your Vision" — is vague, consumer-coded, and doesn't say what SFI does). Must communicate: they're commercial (not residential), full WI + Northern Illinois service area, independent (manufacturer-agnostic) installation crew, and the industries served. Service pages are intentionally on the backburner until the homepage is approved.

A full homepage copy deck (SEO metadata, hero options, all 7 services rewritten with updated SEO-friendly names, industries section, trust section, 4-step process, 13-Q&A FAQ in AEO format, footer copy, dev checklist, LocalBusiness + FAQPage JSON-LD schema) already exists in the local WordPress repo at `homepage-copy-deck.md` — see [[Systems Furniture Installations Stack]]. Wireframes reviewed with the client 2026-06-26 ([Figma](https://www.figma.com/proto/30k2H9BlWSRFtcJUyDqPoh/SFI?node-id=17-4)); client pushed back on leading with "Illinois" in the header and wants service-area messaging visual rather than spelled out in the utility bar.

**Open decision:** Hero H1 — Option A ("Commercial Furniture Installation Across Wisconsin") vs. Option B ("Wisconsin's Commercial Furniture Installation Company") — needs a client call or A/B test.
