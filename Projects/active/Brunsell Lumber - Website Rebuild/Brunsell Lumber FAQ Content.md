**Do you install kitchen countertops and cabinets?**
We do not provide cabinetry or countertop installation. However, we do offer on-site measuring and professional design services to help you create the perfect layout.

**Do you make custom butcher-block tops, or are you able to order them?**
Yes, we do make custom butcher block tops. If you have an odd-sized tabletop or need a custom size, our Millshop can help match almost any size.

**What's the difference between stock, semi-custom, and custom cabinets?**
The main difference between stock, semi-custom, and custom cabinets is the level of flexibility, personalization, and craftsmanship available. Stock cabinets are pre-manufactured in standard sizes, styles, and finishes. They are typically the most budget-friendly option and work well for straightforward layouts where customization is limited. Semi-custom cabinets offer more options for personalization, including additional sizes, finishes, storage solutions, and design features. They provide a balance of flexibility, quality, and value for many kitchen and bath projects. Custom cabinets are built specifically for your space and allow for the highest level of personalization. From unique dimensions and specialty storage solutions to custom finishes and details, they are designed to maximize functionality and create a truly one-of-a-kind space. Our team can help you determine which option best fits your style, needs, and budget while creating a design that works beautifully for your home.

**What's the difference between frameless, framed, and inset cabinets?**
The main difference between frameless, framed, and inset cabinets is the way the cabinet box and doors are constructed, which affects the overall look, storage capacity, and style of your kitchen or bath. Framed cabinets feature a face frame attached to the front of the cabinet box, providing additional structure and a more traditional appearance. The hinges attach to the frame, and the doors can be either overlay or inset depending on the style you choose. Frameless cabinets (also known as European-style cabinets) are constructed without a face frame, creating a clean, modern look with full-access storage. The streamlined design allows for slightly more interior space and a contemporary appearance. Inset cabinets feature doors and drawers that fit flush inside the cabinet frame rather than sitting on top of it. This creates a precise, furniture-like appearance with clean lines and a high level of craftsmanship. Each construction style offers its own benefits, and our designers can help you choose the option that best fits your home's style, storage needs, and budget.

**How do I choose the right countertop for my lifestyle?**
Choosing the right countertop starts with understanding how you use your space. Consider factors such as durability, maintenance, style, and budget to find the best fit for your kitchen or bath. Quartz countertops are a popular choice for busy households because they are durable, low-maintenance, and resistant to stains and scratches. They offer a wide variety of colors and patterns while requiring no sealing. Natural stone countertops, such as granite or quartzite, provide unique beauty and character with each slab. They are durable and heat-resistant but may require periodic maintenance depending on the material. Wood countertops and butcher blocks add warmth, texture, and a natural element to your space. They are a great option for creating a welcoming feel and can provide a functional work surface with proper care. Our designers can help you compare materials, finishes, and features to select a countertop that not only complements your design but also fits the way you live every day.

**Do you install countertops?**
Brunsell Lumber does not provide countertop installation services. However, many of our countertop suppliers (excluding laminate and wood countertops) offer professional on-site measuring, fabrication, and installation services to ensure a seamless fit and quality finished product.

**What affects the cost of new cabinets?**
The cost of new cabinets depends on several factors, including the size of your project, the style and construction of the cabinets, materials selected, and the level of customization. Key factors that can impact pricing include cabinet construction (such as framed, frameless, or inset styles), wood species and finishes, door styles, storage accessories, hardware, and specialty features. The complexity of your layout, the number of cabinets needed, and any unique design requirements can also influence the overall investment. Our team will help you explore options that fit your style, needs, and budget while creating a functional design that maximizes your space. We'll guide you through the available choices so you can make informed decisions with confidence.

**How long does it take to receive cabinets after ordering?**
Lead times vary depending on the cabinetry line, product selections, and current availability. However, the typical lead time for most cabinetry orders is approximately 4–8 weeks. Our team will provide updated timelines during the design and ordering process to help you plan your project accordingly.

**How long does it take for countertops to be fabricated and installed?**
Countertop installation timelines can vary depending on the material selected, project details, and current availability. However, the typical lead time is approximately 3 weeks from the on-site measurement and templating appointment through fabrication and final installation. Our team will help coordinate the process and keep you informed of the expected timeline for your project.
