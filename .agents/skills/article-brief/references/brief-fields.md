# Brief Fields

Use these fields to make article production repeatable.

## Minimum Viable Brief

```text
Topic:
Working title:
Audience:
Article type:
Reader intent:
Thesis or promise:
Business purpose:
Desired action:
Evidence available:
Evidence needed:
Voice constraints:
Format constraints:
Assumptions:
Open questions:
```

## Search Fields

Use only when search visibility matters:

```text
Primary keyword:
Secondary queries:
Search intent:
Competing formats:
Freshness needs:
Internal links:
Metadata need:
```

## Handoff Readiness

A brief is ready when:

- Audience and intent are clear.
- Article type is selected.
- Required evidence is named.
- Unsupported claims are not presented as facts.
- Open questions are visible.
- Next production step is explicit.
