# SEO Ecommerce Audit

Audit ecommerce SEO as bounded evidence across product, collection, marketplace,
and template systems. No catalog, CMS, schema, code, or production changes.

## Operating Contract

1. Resolve client/project/prospect, commerce model, catalog scope, markets,
   languages, currencies, inventory/pricing rules, platform, date, and URL set.
2. Load approved catalog, product/collection templates, analytics/GSC, crawl,
   schema, review, feed, Brand, and marketplace evidence.
3. Ask before live provider, marketplace, page, analytics, or catalog reads.
4. Assess product/collection intent, indexability, canonicals, pagination,
   variants, availability, pricing, reviews, schema facts, internal links,
   filters, duplicate/thin risk, and conversion handoffs.
5. Label facts, first-party records, provider observations, marketplace data,
   vendor studies, heuristics, and recommendations separately.
6. Route schema/code to Development, copy to Writing, merchandising/catalog
   decisions to Strategy or Commerce, and template/crawl work to WordPress.
7. Apply `seo-quality-gate` in `programmatic-seo` mode.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent inventory, price, reviews, offers, product facts, or marketplace data.
- Do not promise rankings, rich results, sales, traffic, or revenue.
- Do not change catalog, feeds, schema, canonicals, URLs, CMS, code, or production.
- Do not recommend scaled pages without the programmatic quality gate.

If this skill conflicts with `.agents/disciplines/seo/rubric.md`, the rubric wins.
