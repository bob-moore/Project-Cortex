# Email Intents

Use `email_intent` to prevent one email style from contaminating another.

## editorial-newsletter

Use for recurring newsletter issues, curated links, commentary, digests, and
relationship-building notes.

Primary job: provide value and build trust.

## promotional-email

Use for offers, launches, sales, registrations, events, webinars, and campaigns.

Primary job: persuade a known audience to take one action.

Allowed: stronger CTA, urgency, offer framing, objection handling, proof.

Not allowed: fake urgency, invented proof, misleading scarcity.

## nurture-email

Use for education, onboarding, trust-building, objections, and consideration.

Primary job: move the reader one step in understanding or confidence.

## announcement

Use for updates, releases, invitations, operational news, or changes.

Primary job: explain what changed and what to do.

## transactional-adjacent

Use for draft-only copy near account, billing, service, support, or operational
email. Keep language precise and low-pressure.

This copy often needs human, legal, or platform review before use.
