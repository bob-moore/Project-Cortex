# Subject And Preview

Use subject and preview text as a pair.

## Subject Rules

- Keep the point clear.
- Prefer 35-60 characters unless the channel specifies otherwise.
- Avoid clickbait.
- Avoid fake urgency.
- Use personalization only when real data exists.
- Match the email body.

## Preview Rules

- Extend the subject.
- Do not repeat the subject.
- Keep it useful when truncated.
- Avoid unsupported claims.

## Patterns

Editorial:

- `This week's practical read on [topic]`
- `[Topic]: what changed and what to watch`

Promotional:

- `[Offer] closes [real date]`
- `A better way to [outcome]`

Nurture:

- `The mistake that makes [problem] harder`
- `Before choosing [solution], check this`

Announcement:

- `[Product/service] update: [specific change]`
- `New dates for [event]`

## Risk Checks

Flag subject lines that:

- Promise results the body cannot support.
- Use deadline language without a real deadline.
- Sound like phishing.
- Hide material terms.
- Conflict with brand voice.
