# Email Structures

Use these structures as starting points.

## Editorial Newsletter

```text
Subject:
Preview:
Opening note:
Main story or insight:
Curated links or sections:
Commentary:
Soft CTA:
Signoff:
```

Commentary should add context, not merely describe links.

## Promotional Email

```text
Subject:
Preview:
Hook:
Reader problem or moment:
Offer:
Benefits:
Proof:
Objection or risk reversal:
CTA:
P.S.:
```

Use one primary CTA.

## Nurture Email

```text
Subject:
Preview:
Useful idea:
Why it matters:
Example:
Next step:
CTA:
```

Teach before asking for too much.

## Announcement

```text
Subject:
Preview:
What changed:
Why it matters:
Details:
What to do:
CTA:
```

## Transactional-Adjacent

```text
Subject:
Opening:
Essential information:
Required action:
Support path:
```

Avoid persuasion unless explicitly approved.
