# Email Safety

Use this reference for email risk checks.

## Approval Boundary

Drafting is allowed inside the writing workflow. Sending, scheduling, importing
contacts, changing automations, and updating email platform settings require
explicit external mutation approval.

## Deliverability Notes

Do not claim deliverability compliance from copy alone. Copy can reduce obvious
risks, but deliverability depends on list quality, domain reputation, consent,
authentication, cadence, and platform configuration.

## Compliance Notes

Flag needs for human review when email involves:

- Legal, medical, financial, insurance, tax, employment, or regulated claims.
- Account, billing, policy, or service change notices.
- Sweepstakes, discounts, deadlines, or conditions.
- Unsubscribe, consent, or privacy language.

## Copy Risks

Avoid:

- Misleading subject lines.
- Fake reply framing.
- Hidden terms.
- Unsupported urgency.
- Unsupported savings or earnings claims.
- Over-personalization without data.
