# Social Channel Operations

Use this skill for the operating layer around organic social channels.

Writer owns post copy through `social-shortform`. Designer owns visual assets.
Marketing owns channel strategy, cadence, calendar shape, distribution
planning, engagement approach, and measurement.

## Operating Contract

1. Resolve client, audience, channels, goals, cadence, resources, and approval
   boundary.
2. Read Brand, Voice, and Stack when client-specific.
3. Identify whether the task is strategy, calendar, audit, engagement plan,
   graphics spec, analytics readout, or handoff.
4. Do not post, schedule, reply, DM, scrape, change profiles, import contacts,
   or mutate external social platforms without explicit approval.
5. Route copy to `social-shortform`, visual assets to Designer, tracking to
   `utm-builder` or `marketing-analytics`, and implementation to the owning
   role.

## Channel Planning

Define for each channel:

- channel role in the marketing system
- audience moment and intent
- content pillars
- format mix
- cadence
- distribution and engagement rhythm
- success metrics
- owner and handoff requirements

## Platform Defaults

Use these only as starting assumptions:

| Platform | Best Fit | Common Formats |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | B2B, founder POV, thought leadership, hiring, partnerships. | Text posts, carousels, documents, short video. |
| X/Twitter | Tech, commentary, community, real-time ideas. | Posts, threads, replies, clips. |
| Instagram | Visual brands, local businesses, lifestyle, proof. | Reels, carousels, stories, grid posts. |
| TikTok | Discovery, short-form education, personality-led brands. | Short videos, hooks, trends. |
| Facebook | Local/community businesses and groups. | Posts, groups, native video, events. |
| Pinterest | Lifestyle, ecommerce, durable discovery. | Vertical pins, idea pins. |
| YouTube Shorts | Video discovery and long-form funnel support. | Shorts, clips, hooks. |
