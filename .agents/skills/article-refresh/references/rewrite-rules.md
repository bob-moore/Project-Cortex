# Rewrite Rules

Use these rules during an article refresh.

## Preserve

Keep:

- Original insight.
- First-hand evidence.
- Useful examples.
- Valid sources.
- Working structure.
- Approved voice.
- Internal links that still fit.

## Change

Change:

- Stale statistics.
- Broken or weak claims.
- Generic introduction.
- Misleading headings.
- Unsupported first-hand language.
- Dead CTAs.
- Metadata that no longer matches the article.

## Avoid

- Turning a narrow update into a full rewrite without approval.
- Adding SEO machinery unrelated to the article goal.
- Replacing concrete details with generic prose.
- Strengthening claims during cleanup.
- Removing caveats that protect accuracy.

## Claim Handling

Run or recommend `claim-check` when the article includes public claims,
statistics, comparisons, or high-stakes material.
