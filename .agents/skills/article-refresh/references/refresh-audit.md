# Refresh Audit

Use this checklist before rewriting an existing article.

## Audit Areas

- Reader intent still clear.
- Thesis still useful.
- Article type still fits.
- Claims are supported.
- Time-sensitive facts are current.
- Headings match section content.
- Internal links still make sense.
- CTA still fits the article.
- Voice fits current Brand and Voice notes.
- Metadata accurately summarizes visible content.

## Findings

Classify findings:

- `keep`: works as-is.
- `minor-edit`: needs light cleanup.
- `verify`: needs source check.
- `replace`: stale or unsupported.
- `cut`: no longer useful.
- `add`: missing section or context.
- `ask`: operator input needed.

## Refresh Verdict

Use:

- `minor edit`: surface cleanup only.
- `refresh`: targeted update.
- `rewrite`: article structure or evidence base needs material rebuild.
- `blocked`: cannot proceed without source, approval, or missing context.
