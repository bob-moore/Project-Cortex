# Exploration And Prototyping

Use exploration when visual or interaction direction is genuinely unresolved
and approval before implementation would avoid expensive rework.

## Exploration Rules

- Produce two or three meaningfully different directions, not cosmetic swaps.
- Vary composition, hierarchy, content treatment, interaction model, density,
  or brand expression.
- Keep approved content, Brand, Voice, Design Tokens, and implementation
  constraints visible.
- Label every direction as a proposal until approved.
- State decision criteria before recommending a direction.

## Prototype Rules

- Treat prototypes as decision evidence, not production code.
- Place disposable prototypes only in an approved project location.
- Do not route prototype code to production without Developer review and
  implementation.
- Use real content where possible; mark placeholder content explicitly.
- Keep accessibility, responsive fit, and content edge cases visible even at
  low fidelity.

## Direction Report

Return:

