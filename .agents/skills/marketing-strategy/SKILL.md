# Marketing Strategy

Use this skill to decide how the marketing system should win.

## Operating Contract

1. Resolve the business objective, ICP, offer, market, funnel stage, channels,
   constraints, and time horizon.
2. Ground strategy in Brand, Voice, Stack, market research, keyword research,
   competitor analysis, analytics, and customer language.
3. Make tradeoffs explicit. Strategy is not a list of tactics.
4. Treat social and email as core strategic channels when relevant, but route
   finished drafts to Writer and operations to future channel-ops skills.
5. Fold free-tool and growth-loop strategy into this skill.
6. Record automation as a deferred capacity gap unless explicitly approved and
   scoped elsewhere.

## Strategy Surfaces

- ICP and segmentation
- positioning and category
- messaging hierarchy and proof strategy
- channel role and cadence for content, search, email, social, YouTube,
  referrals, partnerships, and free tools
- growth loops and product-led growth assumptions
- offer and lead-magnet strategy
- funnel priorities and CRO themes
- measurement plan and UTM convention

## Procedure

1. State the strategic problem and what decision is needed.
2. Summarize the current evidence and missing evidence.
3. Define ICP, buying triggers, pains, desired outcomes, and alternatives.
4. Establish positioning: category, differentiated value, proof, objections,
   and anti-fit.
5. Choose the channel system:
   - Search/content for discoverability.
   - Email/newsletter for trust, nurture, and owned audience.
   - Social for distribution, point-of-view, community, and signal testing.
   - YouTube when video search, education, or creator-led trust matters.
   - Free tools when prospects need a calculator, audit, generator, template,
     or diagnostic before buying.
6. Prioritize 3-5 strategic bets and identify next owner.
