# SEO Content-brief Onboarding Intake

Use this intake before producing a durable SEO content brief when the client,
project, or prospect context is not already complete.

The grilling skill asks one question at a time, gives a recommended answer, and
waits for the User's decision before moving to the next question. Capture the
final answers in the appropriate onboarding context, not only in the brief.

## Intake Order

### 1. Context

- Which client, project, or prospect owns this work?
- Where should the durable brief live?
- Is this a new page or an existing-page refresh?

### 2. Business

- What does the business sell or provide?
- What business outcome should this page support?
- What offer, service, product, or topic has priority?
- What action should the visitor take after using the page?

### 3. Audience

- Who is the intended audience?
- What situation, problem, or job brings them to the page?
- What do they already know?
- What would make the page useful and credible to them?

### 4. Market

- What geographic market or service area matters?
- What language and regional terminology should be used?
- Are there audience, accessibility, legal, regulatory, or compliance constraints?

### 5. Brand And Voice

- Which Brand and Voice notes are authoritative?
- What positioning or differentiator must the page reinforce?
- What proof, expertise, first-hand experience, or approved claims are available?

### 6. Page

For a new page:

- What page type should be considered?
- What topic, service, offer, or user question should it address?
- Is there an existing related page that should be preserved or linked?

For a refresh:

- What is the existing URL?
- What prompted the refresh?
- What must be preserved?
- What has changed in the offer, audience, business, or evidence?

### 7. Search Opportunity

- What search, discovery, or visibility outcome matters?
- Is there a known query, keyword, topic, or user prompt?
- Should additional opportunity research be offered?
- Which approved sources may be used for this request?

Only use providers the User identifies as installed or configured. Ask before
manual external search, current search-result inspection, or competitor-page
analysis.

### 8. Evidence And Measurement

- Is GA/GSC data available for this client/project?
- Is Google Keyword Planner available?
- Has the User configured DataForSEO, SE Ranking, or another provider for this
  work?
- Are there existing audits, content inventories, analytics exports, or source
  packets?
- What date range, market, language, and device should research use?

### 9. Handoff

- Should the Writing handoff use `article-brief`, `web-copy`, or
  `article-refresh`?
- What should Writing know before drafting?
- Which claims require `claim-check`?
- Who approves the brief before writing begins?

### 10. Review And Storage

- Is this brief for internal strategy, client review, or direct Writing handoff?
- Does it need a Home review-queue link?
- What status should it receive after review?

## Completion Rule

The intake is complete when the required business, audience, market, page,
objective, context, source, and handoff decisions are recorded. Then produce the
SEO brief and apply `seo-quality-gate`.
