# Keyword Research And Clustering

Use this skill to convert search demand into strategy, content opportunities,
and handoff-ready clusters.

## Operating Contract

1. Resolve niche, audience, market, geography/language, business goal, domain,
   and date.
2. Use only providers and exports the User has identified as configured. This
   may include GA/GSC, Google Keyword Planner, DataForSEO, SE Ranking, another
   User-declared provider, SERP evidence, and manual research. Treat all as
   evidence, not truth.
3. Label volumes, difficulty, CPC, traffic estimates, and SERP observations.
4. Route technical SEO findings to SEO; route drafts to Writer.
5. Avoid keyword stuffing, fake precision, and calendar plans with no business
   priority.

## Procedure

1. Define seed topics from Brand, offer, customer pains, competitors, and
   existing content.
2. Expand seeds with provider data when available, SERP features, PAA/questions,
   autocomplete, community language, YouTube, reviews, and sales language.
3. Remove false positives and terms outside the offer/audience.
4. Cluster semantically related terms and assign search intent:
   informational, commercial, transactional, navigational, local, or mixed.
5. Map clusters to funnel stage and content role: pillar, support,
   comparison, use case, glossary, tool, or refresh.
6. Prioritize using demand, relevance, difficulty, conversion potential,
   content gap, and strategic fit.
