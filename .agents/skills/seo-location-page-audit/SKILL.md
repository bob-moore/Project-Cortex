# SEO Location Page Audit

Audit location and service-area pages for useful local value and legitimate
business purpose. Do not create, edit, publish, or delete pages here.

## Operating Contract

1. Resolve client/project/prospect, business type, locations, service areas,
   market, language, URL scope, query scope, and audit date.
2. Load approved business records, local/maps/citation/review artifacts, crawl
   evidence, page content, internal-link data, analytics/GSC, and Brand context.
3. Ask before live external provider or page reads.
4. Assess location fact accuracy, unique local usefulness, service-area intent,
   page purpose, query/page fit, content quality, internal links, conversion
   paths, schema facts, duplication, thinness, and doorway risk.
5. Label source, date, evidence type, confidence, and content limitations.
6. Recommend keep, improve, merge, redirect-review, noindex-review, or retire-
   review only with evidence and owner; do not make the change.
7. Route copy to Writing, schema/code to Development, URL/CMS actions to
   WordPress, and location/business decisions to Strategy.
8. Apply `seo-quality-gate` in `local-seo` mode.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent locations, addresses, service areas, hours, staff, or offers.
- Do not recommend doorway pages whose only purpose is capturing queries.
- Do not change URLs, redirects, canonicals, indexability, schema, CMS, code,
  copy, or production.
- Do not promise local rankings, traffic, leads, calls, or revenue.
