# Design Research Reference Sources

Use this as a routed source map, not a flat gallery. Confirm current URL,
retrieval status, and licensing before recording a reference.

## Local-business and service structure

| Source | URL | Use | Bias/risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Webflow Home Services | https://webflow.com/templates/search?query=home%20services | Home-service page structures, quotes, services, proof, service areas, forms | Template repetition, stock imagery, Webflow lock-in |
| Squarespace Professional Services | https://www.squarespace.com/templates/browse/topic/professional-services?vertical=DOMESTIC_SERVICES | Restrained professional/local-service layouts and content hierarchy | Lifestyle/consulting bias; platform lock-in |
| Astra templates | https://wpastra.com/website-templates/ | WordPress local-business and service starters | Theme/builder lock-in; quality varies |
| WordPress Showcase | https://wordpress.org/showcase/ | Real WordPress production structures | Larger/design-led organizations overrepresented |
| WordPress Patterns | https://wordpress.org/patterns/ | Copyable Gutenberg sections and implementation patterns | Pattern library, not finished art direction |
| Awwwards Services | https://www.awwwards.com/websites/services/ | Real service-business art direction and photography | Award bias; may sacrifice speed/conversion |

## Selection rule

A typical local PPC research packet should combine three local/service references,
two conversion references, one implementation reference, and one visual/editorial
stretch reference. Record what each contributes and what must not be copied.
