# Competitor Analysis

Use this skill for competitive intelligence that supports strategy, positioning,
content planning, sales enablement, and market opportunity decisions.

## Operating Contract

1. Resolve the client's offer, ICP, market, competitor set, and purpose.
2. Classify competitors as direct, indirect, substitute/status quo, adjacent, or
   emerging.
3. Use primary sources when possible: competitor sites, pricing pages,
   product docs, public reviews, job posts, press, search results, and client
   win/loss evidence.
4. Acknowledge competitor strengths and client limitations.
5. Do not invent feature, pricing, customer, funding, traffic, or review claims.
6. Route finished `vs`/alternative page copy to Writer and SEO readiness to SEO.

## Procedure

1. Define the decision: positioning, market entry, battlecard, comparison page,
   sales enablement, pricing, or feature prioritization.
2. Build the competitor set and classify each competitor.
3. Gather evidence across product, pricing, positioning, traction, reviews,
   content, and team/investment signals.
4. Identify market gaps: underserved segments, unclaimed positions, price
   gaps, feature gaps, onboarding/service gaps, and trust gaps.
5. Synthesize implications for positioning, channels, content, CRO, and sales.
