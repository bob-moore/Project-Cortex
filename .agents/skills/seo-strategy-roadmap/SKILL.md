# SEO Strategy Roadmap

Turn approved SEO evidence into a prioritized, sequenced strategy and execution
roadmap. The roadmap coordinates SEO, Strategy, Writing, Development,
WordPress, and review roles without claiming ownership of their deliverables.

## Operating Contract

1. Resolve client/project/prospect, domain, business goal, offer, audience,
   market, language, device, current state, planning horizon, and capacity.
2. Load approved keyword research, SERP intent, content audit, topic cluster,
   technical/page audit, analytics, and business-context artifacts.
3. Separate observed evidence, first-party data, provider estimates, heuristics,
   hypotheses, assumptions, recommendations, and decisions.
4. Translate evidence into initiatives with a clear objective, affected assets,
   owner role, dependency, priority, effort band, review point, and verification
   method.
5. Prioritize business relevance, user value, intent/conversion fit, evidence
   strength, strategic leverage, feasibility, effort, and dependency order.
6. Sequence work into Now, Next, Later, or recurring monitoring based on the
   approved planning horizon. Do not treat the sequence as a guarantee.
7. Route final copy to Writing, code and technical implementation to Development,
   CMS changes to WordPress, and approval/verification to the appropriate role.
8. Define measurement and recheck methods without promising ranking, traffic,
   revenue, conversion, or AI-visibility outcomes.
9. Apply `seo-quality-gate` in `reporting` or the relevant SEO mode.

## Boundaries

- Do not create a roadmap from unsupported metrics or assumed client priorities.
- Do not convert volume, difficulty, ranking, traffic, or provider scores into
  outcome guarantees.
- Do not write final copy, metadata prose, code, CMS changes, or publishing
  instructions as completed work.
- Do not silently reprioritize settled business decisions.
- Do not bypass Writing, Development, WordPress, verifier, or approval gates.
- Do not treat Now/Next/Later as a promise of delivery or ranking outcome.
