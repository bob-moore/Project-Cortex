# Section Contracts

Use section contracts to make outlines useful for drafting.

## Standard Section

```text
Heading:
Reader question:
Answer-first direction:
Points:
Evidence:
Example:
Visual:
Internal link:
Risk:
```

## Evidence Notes

Mark evidence as:

- available.
- needed.
- user-provided.
- source-backed.
- assumed.

Do not turn evidence needs into claims.

## Flow Checks

- Each section earns its place.
- Each section answers a distinct reader need.
- Section order follows reader logic.
- H3s clarify, not decorate.
- Conclusion does not introduce a new argument.
