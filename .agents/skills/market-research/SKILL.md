# Market Research

Use this skill to turn market evidence into a bounded opportunity view.

## Operating Contract

1. Resolve client, market, geography, audience, decision, and date range.
2. Read Brand, Voice, and Stack when client-specific.
3. Label every source as `authority`, `source-fact`, `client-data`,
   `provider-estimate`, `judged`, `assumed`, or `blocked`.
4. Use current sources for time-sensitive market facts.
5. Do not present TAM, trend, social, search, or YouTube numbers as exact.
6. Route finished copy to Writer and implementation to the owning role.

## Modes

- `market-sizing`: TAM/SAM/SOM, bottom-up validation, assumption ledger.
- `customer-research`: segments, pains, jobs-to-be-done, buyer journey,
  interview or review language.
- `landscape`: market categories, trends, substitutes, forces, and risks.
- `opportunity`: gap analysis, prioritization, channel fit, next bets.
- `youtube-research`: YouTube videos, channels, transcripts, comments, and
  audience patterns as demand evidence.

## Procedure

1. Define the research question and what decision the answer should support.
2. Gather evidence from the narrowest useful source set: client data, customer
   language, public sources, reports, competitors, communities, YouTube, or
   search evidence.
3. Separate the market into customer segments, use cases, alternatives, and
   buying triggers.
4. Size or rank the opportunity with explicit assumptions.
5. Identify risks, unknowns, and what would change the recommendation.
6. Return a decision-ready artifact.
