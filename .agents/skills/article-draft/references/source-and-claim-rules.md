# Source And Claim Rules

Use this reference for factual discipline while drafting.

## Public Claims

Public statistics, comparisons, rankings, and performance claims need support.

Include:

- Source name.
- Link or vault path.
- Date or study period when relevant.
- Caveat or scope limitation when needed.

## Unsupported Claims

When support is missing:

- Mark as an open claim.
- Soften to qualitative language.
- Ask for proof.
- Remove the claim.

Do not keep an unsourced statistic because it sounds useful.

## First-Hand Claims

Use first-hand language only when the operator supplies experience, method,
result, or observation.

Unsupported phrases to avoid:

- "we tested"
- "our research shows"
- "in our experience"
- "customers told us"

## Source Freshness

Current or changing topics need current sources. If source freshness matters and
external read is unavailable, mark the draft provisional.
