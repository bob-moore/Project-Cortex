# Claim Types

Use these claim types in claim-check reports.

## Statistical

Numbers, percentages, money, counts, multipliers, rankings, estimates, and
benchmarks.

Examples:

- "Cuts onboarding time by 40%."
- "Ranked number one."
- "Used by 2,000 teams."

## Comparative

Claims that compare one option to another.

Examples:

- "Faster than manual reporting."
- "More affordable than hiring in-house."
- "The safest option."

## Capability

Claims about what a product, service, process, person, or organization can do.

Examples:

- "Integrates with QuickBooks."
- "Same-day estimates are available."
- "Works with all major CRMs."

## Outcome

Claims about results a reader may get.

Examples:

- "Generate more qualified leads."
- "Reduce missed appointments."
- "Improve close rates."

## Proof And Trust

Claims about testimonials, credentials, awards, certifications, partnerships,
case studies, customer names, media mentions, or authority.

## High-Stakes

Claims involving legal, medical, financial, employment, compliance, safety,
insurance, tax, platform policy, or regulated advice.

High-stakes claims require current source support or explicit user-provided
authority. Mark unsupported high-stakes claims as blockers.
