# Derivative Safety

Use this reference before returning repurposed outputs.

## Claim Safety

- Preserve scope.
- Preserve caveats.
- Preserve attribution.
- Do not turn one example into a universal claim.
- Do not convert qualitative support into a number.

## Copyright Safety

- Summarize source ideas in new structure.
- Avoid copying long passages.
- Use short quotes only when necessary and attributable.
- Do not mirror a third-party article's section sequence when creating a new
  article.

## Context Safety

- Keep audience and channel fit clear.
- Do not make community posts sound like ads unless promotion is allowed.
- Do not imply endorsement from a quoted person or source.
- Do not detach a claim from limitations that made it true.
