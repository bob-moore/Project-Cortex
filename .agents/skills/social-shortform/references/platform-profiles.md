# Platform Profiles

Use platform profiles to shape output. Treat exact limits and UI behavior as
subject to change; verify when precision matters.

## LinkedIn

Best for professional insight, founder/operator perspective, B2B education,
case notes, and discussion.

Use:

- Strong first line.
- Short paragraphs.
- Concrete point of view.
- One takeaway or argument.
- Discussion question when useful.

## X Post

Best for compact observations, links, sharp takes, and quick updates.

Use:

- One idea.
- Minimal setup.
- No filler.
- Optional link or CTA.

## X Thread

Best for multi-step argument, list, teardown, or lesson sequence.

Use:

- Numbered posts.
- One idea per post.
- Standalone value in each post.
- Clear closing.

## Instagram Caption

Best when copy supports a visual.

Use:

- First-line hook.
- Short paragraphs.
- Clear visual context.
- CTA that fits the post.

## Shorts Script

Best for short vertical video scripts.

Use:

- 0-3 second hook.
- Fast setup.
- 3-5 beats.
- On-screen text suggestions.
- CTA appropriate to the video.

## Community Prompt

Best for Slack, Discord, Reddit-style discussion.

Use:

- Direct context.
- Useful question.
- Disclosure when linked to owned content.
- Low promotional pressure.
