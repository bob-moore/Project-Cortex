# Standard Pages

Use this reference for ordinary website pages where the main job is orientation,
trust, and clarity.

## Standard Page Rules

- Lead with what the reader came to understand.
- Keep the tone useful and grounded.
- Explain the next step without pressure.
- Use proof to establish credibility, not to over-sell.
- Prefer helpful specificity over conversion theatrics.
- Keep CTAs clear and proportional.

## Common CTA Styles

Use CTAs that fit normal browsing behavior:

- Contact us.
- Get an estimate.
- View services.
- See examples.
- Meet the team.
- Check availability.
- Request information.
- Compare options.

Avoid aggressive CTAs unless the page has promotional intent.

## About Page Notes

About pages should answer:

- Who is behind this?
- What do they believe or do differently?
- Why should the reader trust them?
- Is this a good fit?

Avoid opening with generic founding stories unless they matter to reader trust.

## Service Page Notes

Service pages should answer:

- What problem does the service solve?
- Who is it for?
- What is included?
- How does the process work?
- What result or relief can the reader reasonably expect?
- What is the next step?

Keep persuasion present but restrained. Service pages often need confidence more
than urgency.

## Homepage Notes

Homepages need routing discipline.

- Make the first screen clear.
- Show the primary paths.
- Use concise proof.
- Avoid trying to explain every service in full.
- Send deeper detail to service, product, or case-study pages.
