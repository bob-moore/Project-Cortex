# Promotional Pages

Use this reference for landing pages, sales pages, campaign pages, and other
conversion-focused web copy.

## Promotional Page Rules

- Focus on one audience and one action.
- Make the promise concrete.
- Show why the reader should act.
- Use proof before pressure.
- Repeat the CTA when the page is long.
- Answer objections directly.
- Make urgency truthful or omit it.

## Offer Frame

Clarify before drafting:

```text
Audience:
Buying moment:
Offer:
Outcome:
Mechanism:
Proof:
Objections:
Risk reversal:
Primary CTA:
Urgency source:
```

## Persuasion Tools

Use when supported:

- Direct promise.
- Pain and cost of inaction.
- Before/after contrast.
- Specific benefits.
- Testimonials or case studies.
- Demonstration.
- Comparison to alternatives.
- FAQ and objection handling.
- Guarantee or risk reversal.
- Real deadline or capacity constraint.

## Urgency Rules

Allowed:

- Real registration deadline.
- Real event date.
- Real limited capacity.
- Real price change.
- Real bonus expiration.
- Real seasonal timing.

Not allowed:

- Fake countdown.
- "Limited spots" without capacity evidence.
- Implied scarcity because it sounds persuasive.
- Fear language beyond the real risk.

## Sales Copy Without Hype

Use stronger persuasion, not inflated language.

Prefer:

- "Book a 20-minute consult to see whether this is a fit."
- "Enrollment closes Friday because onboarding starts Monday."
- "See the exact process before committing."
- "Built for local service teams that need more qualified calls."

Avoid:

- "Transform your business overnight."
- "The ultimate solution."
- "Guaranteed to skyrocket results."
- "This changes everything."

## Promotional Quality Check

Before handoff, check:

- One primary action.
- Promise matches evidence.
- Proof appears near major claims.
- Objections are addressed.
- Urgency is real or absent.
- CTA is visible and repeated appropriately.
- Claims do not exceed the approval source.
