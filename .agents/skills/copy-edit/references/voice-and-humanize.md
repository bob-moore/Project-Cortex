# Voice And Humanize

Use this reference for voice passes and drafts that read generic, model-shaped,
or over-polished.

## Voice Source Priority

1. Direct user instruction.
2. Client Voice note.
3. Client Brand note.
4. Approved examples in the vault.
5. Draft's own strongest natural passages.
6. Judged editorial voice.

Do not invent a brand voice when none exists. Mark judged voice decisions.

## Voice Checks

Check:

- Formality level.
- Sentence rhythm.
- Preferred terms.
- Forbidden terms.
- Humor or directness.
- Use of contractions.
- Punctuation habits.
- Local or industry vocabulary.
- Reading level.

## Model-Shaped Patterns

Remove or rewrite:

- Generic openings such as "in today's fast-paced world".
- Forced contrast pairs such as "not only X but also Y".
- Inflated phrases such as "plays a crucial role" or "serves as a testament".
- Vague transitions such as "additionally", "moreover", and "furthermore" when
  they do no real work.
- Stacked -ing clauses that add fake depth.
- Empty hype such as "powerful", "seamless", "robust", "innovative", or
  "game-changing".
- Chat residue such as "let's dive in", "great question", and "hope this helps".

## Humanize Without Drift

Humanizing means making the draft more specific, natural, and voice-fit. It does
not mean adding unsupported anecdotes, jokes, slang, contractions, or emotional
claims.

Use:

- Concrete nouns.
- Direct verbs.
- Specific transitions.
- Normal spoken cadence where the voice allows it.
- Varied sentence length.
- Examples already supported by the source context.

Avoid:

- Making serious content casual by default.
- Removing necessary precision.
- Replacing a clear technical term with a vague common word.
- Adding personality that conflicts with the brand.

## Read-Aloud Pass

Run a read-aloud style pass for rhythm:

- Mark places where the sentence trips.
- Remove repeated sentence openings.
- Split sentences that carry too many turns.
- Merge choppy sentences when the logic belongs together.
- Keep memorable lines only when they remain clear.
