# Editing Sweeps

Use sequential sweeps when editing anything longer than a few lines. Avoid
trying to solve every issue in one pass.

## Sweep 1: Intent

Confirm the draft answers the core task.

- Identify the audience.
- Identify the desired action or takeaway.
- Identify the channel and mode.
- Identify the facts, claims, or phrasing that must survive.

If intent is unclear, ask or mark the edit provisional.

## Sweep 2: Structure

Make the artifact easy to follow.

- Move the main point earlier.
- Reorder sections around reader need.
- Break dense paragraphs.
- Convert lists into bullets where scanability matters.
- Remove tangents that do not serve the contract.

Do not change strategy unless the edit request includes strategic revision.

## Sweep 3: Clarity

Make each sentence easy to understand.

- Resolve unclear pronouns.
- Replace vague nouns with concrete nouns when source context supports it.
- Split overloaded sentences.
- Remove duplicated setup.
- Define or replace jargon based on audience sophistication.

## Sweep 4: Voice

Apply the known voice source.

- Match Brand and Voice notes.
- Match approved examples from the vault.
- Preserve intentional local or client-specific language.
- Smooth tone shifts between sections.

When no voice source exists, label voice changes as judged.

## Sweep 5: Reader Value

Make every claim answer why it matters.

- Convert features into benefits.
- Add connective phrases where the reader needs the implication.
- Remove unsupported superiority.
- Clarify the next step.

## Sweep 6: Proof And Risk

Protect factual integrity.

- Flag unsupported statistics, results, comparisons, guarantees, and
  testimonials.
- Keep claims conservative when evidence is missing.
- Label assumptions and open items.
- Check high-stakes topics against the writing rubric.

## Sweep 7: Rhythm And Polish

Clean the prose after the larger edits are stable.

- Remove filler.
- Vary sentence length.
- Fix punctuation and grammar.
- Remove formulaic transitions.
- Keep formatting proportional to the artifact.

Run this sweep last so surface polish does not hide structural problems.
