# Preservation Rules

Use this reference when editing drafts with facts, claims, quotes, case studies,
testimonials, legal language, pricing, dates, or approved strategy.

## Preserve By Default

Do not silently change:

- Names, titles, company names, product names, and locations.
- Dates, years, durations, prices, percentages, and metrics.
- Quotes and testimonials.
- Legal, medical, financial, regulatory, employment, or compliance language.
- Approved offer terms, guarantees, disclaimers, and calls to action.
- Audience, market, positioning, and strategic recommendation.

## Allowed Without Reapproval

Make these edits when they preserve meaning:

- Fix grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
- Clarify sentence structure.
- Remove filler.
- Break paragraphs.
- Improve transitions.
- Replace vague wording with specific wording already present in source context.
- Convert passive voice to active voice when the actor is known.

## Requires A Flag Or Question

Flag before changing:

- A claim that appears unsupported.
- A result that seems inflated or unverifiable.
- A quote that contains grammar issues but may need exact preservation.
- A statement that could carry legal, medical, financial, or policy meaning.
- A CTA that changes the user's commitment.
- A change that shifts audience, promise, or offer.

## Claim Notes

Use this shape for uncertain claims:

```text
Claim:
Issue:
Evidence available:
Suggested edit:
Needed input:
```

Prefer conservative wording when evidence is weak:

```text
Original: Guaranteed to double conversion.
Safer: Designed to improve conversion, with performance depending on traffic,
offer, and audience fit.
```

Only use the safer version when it still fits the operator's intent. Otherwise
flag the claim and ask.
