# Marketing Discipline

This directory is the canonical discipline layer for general marketing work.

Marketing owns the evidence and decision layer before copy, design,
implementation, or WordPress state changes: market research, keyword research,
competitive intelligence, positioning, channel strategy, funnel analytics, CRO
hypotheses, and attribution conventions.

Writing owns finished copy. SEO owns SEO audit and technical/search evidence
gates. Design, Development, and WordPress Operator own implementation surfaces.

## Files

- `contract.json`: machine-readable discipline contract.
- `modes.md`: marketing modes and ownership boundaries.
- `skill-map.json`: disposition of imported and deferred marketing sources.
- `rubric.md`: canonical marketing quality gate.

## Current Canonical Skills

- `market-research`
- `keyword-research-and-clustering`
- `competitor-analysis`
- `marketing-strategy`
- `marketing-analytics`
- `conversion-rate-optimization`
- `utm-builder`
- `social-channel-operations`
- `newsletter-channel-operations`
- `email-deliverability`
- `email-quality-auditor`

Social and email are core marketing channels. Marketing owns their channel
strategy, cadence, operating plan, quality gates, and analytics. Finished
drafts route to `social-shortform` and `email-newsletter`.

Marketing automation is recorded as a deferred capacity gap because it implies
platform configuration and external mutation risk.
