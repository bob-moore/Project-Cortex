## Scope

Client-facing digital services: web development/design (Bob, solo), PPC, SEO (technical + content), CRO, and client communication/account management (the two DCSMs + AE).

**Client base:** Full `Clients/<Client>/` folders exist for clients with active
build/dev/content engagements or recurring operational relevance. Maintenance
counts move as accounts onboard/offboard; a live WPRemote pull on 2026-07-20
found 54 client sites at that point. Re-run WPRemote when a current count
matters. See `harness/manual.md` for the client-vs-project distinction.

**Realistic near-term growth (per Bob, 2026-07-10):** ~50-60 new client onboardings expected this quarter (Q3 2026). Not all new clients will need active-build tooling, but this is the driver behind scoping safer agent/tool access now rather than later. See [[Agent OS R&D]].

**Parent company context:** Mid-West Family Madison is primarily a **radio company** — Digital is one department within a radio-first organization, not the other way around (per Bob, 2026-07-10).
